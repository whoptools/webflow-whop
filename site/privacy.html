Privacy

Whop Checkout is an independent integration by Yaroslav Shmarov.

The service stores your Webflow site ID and an encrypted Webflow authorization token to install and remove checkout scripts. The Designer’s user token is verified with Webflow when you request an action. Tokens are not included in published sites.

Your plan ID and checkout preferences are stored as attributes on your Webflow elements. Payment details are collected by Whop’s payment components and processed by Whop and its payment providers. This service does not receive card details or store customer orders. It does not add its own analytics or visitor tracking.

Disconnecting removes the stored Webflow authorization for that site. Contact yshmarov@gmail.com for support or data deletion.

The optional Whop signup link contains Yaroslav’s referral code. It does not change attribution on your customers’ purchases.
