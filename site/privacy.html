Whop Checkout for Webflow

Privacy

What the service stores, and what it never sees.

  • Stored: your Webflow site ID and an encrypted Webflow authorization token, used only to install and remove checkout scripts.
  • Verified, not stored: the Designer’s user token, checked with Webflow on each action. Tokens never reach published sites.
  • On your elements: plan ID and checkout settings, as Webflow element attributes.
  • Never seen: card details or customer orders. Whop and its payment providers process payments.
  • No tracking: no analytics or visitor tracking.

Disconnecting deletes the stored authorization for that site. Support or data deletion: yshmarov@gmail.com.
