# Code of conduct

Whop Checkout for Webflow is part of [WhopTools](https://whoptools.com). We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [Yaroslav Shmarov](https://github.com/yshmarov), at yshmarov@gmail.com. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who violate this code, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
